Our Story

She Wanted to Go Out.
Just Not to a Bar.

Café 333 was created by Coralyn Martin, founder of Social Cabaret and The Recovery Revolution.

Six years sober, Coralyn loved going out. She loved music. She loved dressing up. She loved the magic of live performance.

But every night out seemed to end the same way.

The music was too loud to talk.

The drinks kept getting stronger.

And by 10pm the room was full of people who probably should have gone home an hour ago.

She kept thinking:

"I want to go out... but I don't want to go to a bar."

So she built something different.

Kansas City's First
Sober Jazz Bar

Café 333 is built around the idea that a great night out shouldn't require alcohol.

The room is elegant.

The music is professional.

The food is serious.

Live music fills the room without overpowering it. The food is meant to be shared. The drinks are crafted with the same care and creativity you'd expect from any cocktail bar.

Everything here is designed around one simple idea:

Great nights aren't defined by what you drink. They're defined by who you're with.

A night worth remembering.

A listening room.

A complete night out.
