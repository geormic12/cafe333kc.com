Good to Know

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are strongly recommended. The room is intimate and seats close to the stage go quickly, especially on weekends. Reserve your table to guarantee your spot for the evening.

Do you serve alcohol?

No. Café 333 is Kansas City's first sober jazz bar. Every drink is zero proof — crafted with the same care and creativity you'd expect from any cocktail bar, just without the alcohol.

Can I order food without attending a show?

Yes. You're welcome to come in for dinner and drinks during our hours. That said, there's live music every night from 6–10 PM, so the show usually finds you.

What is included with each experience?

It depends on the night you choose. Dinner & A Show includes a reserved table and curated dining around the performance. The Café 333 Experience reserves your table for the full four-hour evening. The No-Brainer Date Night bundles four hours, four specialty drinks, and four plates — everything planned.

How long should I plan to stay?

Plan for the evening. Our experiences are built around roughly four hours of music, food, drinks, and conversation — enjoyed at your own pace. This is a destination, not a quick stop.

Do musicians perform every night?

Yes. Live music plays every night we're open, from 6–10 PM, featuring Kansas City's finest jazz musicians, vocalists, and performers.

Can I host a private event?

Absolutely. Café 333 is a beautiful setting for celebrations, gatherings, and private events. Get in touch and we'll help you plan the night.
